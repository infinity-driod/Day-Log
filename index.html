        Welcome to Tracker
        • Talk or type. Tap the mic, or type. Say several things at once: “an hour on the report, then 30 minutes on email.”
        • Tap an entry to edit it. Tap its circle to mark it finished.
        • Use the stopwatch next to the send arrow to time a task live. Stop or Finish it when you’re done.
        • Find things fast with the quick buttons above the keyboard and the search box in the List tab.
        • Back up now and then. Your entries live only on this phone. Settings has a backup you can restore.
        • Changed your mind? After you log, edit, finish or delete something, tap Undo in the message that pops up.
        • Do it again. Open an entry and tap “Log again today” to repeat it. Log every day to build a streak.
        • New: add notes and #tags to entries, set a weekly goal, lock the app with a PIN, and save a weekly report as an image.
        • Install it. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
